What the record establishes

Not every record carries the same weight

A finding, an admission, a settlement and a study say different things. Each case is counted once, under the kind of record the Casebook found for it.

A finding
A court, regulator or other official body found it.
71
An admission
The company, agency or person involved said so in public.

A settlement
The parties settled. A settlement isn't a finding of fault.
