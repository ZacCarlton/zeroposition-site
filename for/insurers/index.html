For insurers

Check the evidence behind an AI claim before you cover it

When an insured relies on an AI product, the claims made for that product are part of the risk. We examine the evidence behind the claims you name and send you a signed, private report.

What you send

  • The claims the insured, or its AI vendor, makes about the product.
  • The evidence that came with the submission.
  • How the insured uses the product.

What comes back

Two findings on each claim: how far the evidence reaches, and whether any evidence inside the claim's scope shows it's false. Plus what's still open. The report goes only to the party who asked for the check.

What it isn't

It isn't a rating, and we make no claim about premiums. It tells you what the evidence shows, for the scope you named.
