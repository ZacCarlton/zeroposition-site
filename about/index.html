About

Who we are, and why trust us

Zero Position examines the evidence behind AI claims for the people who rely on them. It started in 2026 with a contractor who tried to run his own company on AI.

Why a contractor

Every job I built had to pass an inspector

I'm a developer, design-builder and contractor with more than 200 completed projects. Each one had to pass inspection by someone who doesn't work for me.

In 2026 I started running my own company on AI agents. I logged the mistakes they made and what caught each one. That log is on Our record. Buyers of AI products mostly have the vendor's own test reports to go on.

The company

Zero Position Holdings, LLC, a Kansas company, based in Dodge City, Kansas.

What we won't do

  • Publish a verdict on any named party.
  • Name a vendor beside a failure, anywhere on this site.
  • Charge more for a pass.
  • Call an examination a certification.
  • Send a report to anyone but the party who asked for the check.

SEAT
ExaminerSeat being filled

Signs the findings. Has shown practice checking software or systems against stated criteria: for example, an FAA software designee, an independent verification and validation engineer, or a test and evaluation engineer.

SEAT
Second reviewerSeat being filled

Independent of the examiner. Reads every finding and either releases it or sends it back with the point in dispute.

The examiner and the second reviewer both write down their ties to the vendor, to you and to the deal before the work starts. The vendor can answer written questions and can't edit a finding. None of this makes the examination a certification. It's a written finding on the vendor's evidence, and on what's still open, for the scope you named.
